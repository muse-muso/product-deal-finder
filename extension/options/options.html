Product Deal Finder – Options

How it works

Track product pages on JB Hi-Fi, Officeworks, Amazon AU, The Good Guys, or Harvey Norman. When you visit a tracked page, the extension reads the current price and notifies you if it’s at or below your target. Your list syncs across Firefox installs where you’re signed in. No data is sent to any server.

Import / Export

Export your tracked list as JSON (backup or move to another browser). Import to add or replace tracked products.
