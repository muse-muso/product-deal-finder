Product Deal Finder

Options

How it works

Track product pages on JB Hi-Fi, Officeworks, Amazon AU, The Good Guys, or Harvey Norman. When you visit a tracked page, the extension reads the current price and notifies you if it’s at or below your target. Your list syncs across Firefox installs where you’re signed in. No data is sent to any server.

Notifications

Display

Email price alerts (free)

When a price drops to your target, open a draft email in your default mail app or Gmail/Outlook. You click Send — no server, no cost.

Automatic email via desktop app

If you run the Product Deal Finder desktop app (Windows), enable the relay there and below. The extension will POST alerts to the app, which sends email via your SMTP — fully automatic, $0.

Import / Export

Export your tracked list as JSON (backup or move to another browser). Import to add or replace tracked products.
